# Snake

A slow, friendly snake moves on a grid. Guide it to the fruit (apples, cherries, strawberries and pears): each fruit makes it one cell longer. There is no hurry: the snake only moves while the game is running, and you can pause whenever you like.

## How to play
- Use the **four big arrows** (or swipe on the board) to choose where the snake goes. Pressing an arrow also starts the game.
- **Pause** stops the snake. The game also pauses by itself when you open the settings or leave the app; press **Continue** to go on.
- Your length and your best length for that level are shown. The best length is kept even when you start other games.

## Levels
- **Easy:** very slow. The snake goes through the edges and comes back on the other side, and if it touches itself it simply loses the end of its tail; the game never ends.
- **Medium:** slow. It still goes through the edges, but touching itself ends the game.
- **Hard:** a little faster, and the edges are walls: touching a wall or itself ends the game.

## Tips
- Plan your turns early: the snake keeps up to two arrow presses in mind.
- Moving along the edges keeps the middle free for the snake's long body.
