# Cryptogram

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/cryptogram.md) · [Español](../../es/games/cryptogram.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/cryptogram.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/cryptogram.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/cryptogram.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/cryptogram.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/cryptogram.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/cryptogram.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/cryptogram.md)

A well-known saying is written in code: every letter was swapped for another symbol. Find which letter hides behind each symbol and the saying appears.

## How to play

- Each square shows its symbol in small print below a line. On **Easy** the symbols are numbers (1, 2, 3...); on the other levels they are other letters. A letter never hides behind itself.
- Tap a square: every square with the same symbol lights up. Then tap the letter you think it hides: it is written in all those squares at once.
- Putting a letter that you already used on another symbol moves it here. The ⌫ button takes the letter off the chosen symbol.
- Letters on a green background were revealed and are always right.
- **Hint** writes the right letter on the chosen symbol (or on the first one that is not right yet).
- When every letter is right, the whole saying is shown.

All the sayings are traditional proverbs and old sayings. Accents are not written (É is E); in Spanish Ñ is a letter of its own, in German Ä Ö Ü ß are written AE OE UE SS, in Russian Ё is Е, and Arabic is written without harakat and read from the right.

The cryptogram is played in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Russian and Arabic. Japanese and Chinese are not written with a small alphabet whose letters can be swapped, so in those languages the game first asks which of these languages to play in.

## Levels

- **Easy**: numbers instead of letters, and three letters already revealed.
- **Medium**: letters, one revealed.
- **Hard**: letters, nothing revealed.

## Tips

- Start with short words: a one-letter word in English is almost always A or I, and three-letter words are often THE or AND.
- A symbol that appears many times is probably a vowel or a common letter such as E, T or S.
- Remember well-known sayings: once you see a few letters, the rest often comes by itself.
