# Spider

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/spider.md) · [Español](../../es/games/spider.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/spider.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/spider.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/spider.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/spider.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/spider.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/spider.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/spider.md)

A patience game with two decks (104 cards) on ten columns. Build runs from **King down to Ace in one suit**: as soon as a run is complete it leaves the table on its own. Finish all eight runs to win.

## How to play

- 54 cards are dealt: six in each of the first four columns, five in the others, only the last card of each column face up.
- You can put a card on any card **one rank higher**, whatever its suit (a 6 on any 7). Any card or run can go on an empty column.
- Cards move together only when they are in order **of the same suit**. Tap a card: it moves with the cards on top of it to the best place. Tap it again to try the next place.
- When a face-down card is uncovered, it turns over by itself.
- Tap the **deck** (in the corner on the side of your thumb) to deal a new row: one card on every column. You have five rows. By the classic rule, you can only deal when **no column is empty**: if you try, the game tells you to fill the empty column first.
- **Undo** takes back moves, **Hint** shows a useful move (or tells you to deal). If no useful move is left and the deck is empty, the game says so: you can undo some moves, give up or keep looking.

## The rules you can choose

The number of suits is the difficulty, so there is no separate level to choose.

- **One suit**: all cards are spades. The easiest way to play and the best one to learn.
- **Two suits**: spades and hearts. You have to think more about where each card goes.
- **Four suits**: all four suits. A real challenge: many games cannot be won.

## Tips

- Turning over face-down cards is the most important thing: prefer moves that uncover a card.
- Build on cards of the same suit whenever you can: mixed runs cannot move together.
- An empty column is precious. Use it to sort cards, not just to park one.
- Before dealing a new row, make every useful move you see: the new cards will cover your columns.
