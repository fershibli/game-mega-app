# Korean chess (Janggi)

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/janggi.md) · [Español](../../es/games/janggi.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/janggi.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/janggi.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/janggi.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/janggi.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/janggi.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/janggi.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/janggi.md)

Korean chess is a cousin of Chinese chess, played in Korea for centuries. The board has no river, the palaces have diagonal lines that several pieces can use, and you may **pass** instead of moving.

## The board

- 9 lines across and 10 lines down; the pieces stand **on the crossings**.
- Each side has a **palace** (3 × 3 crossings with an X). Inside the palaces, pieces may also move along the diagonal lines of the X.
- You play **blue** (Cho), at the bottom, and move first. The computer plays red (Han).
- The general starts in the **middle** of its palace.

## The pieces and their marks

Every piece shows its character and small marks showing how it moves:

- a **dot** = one step; **two dots** = the longer jump of the elephant;
- an **arrow** = any distance;
- an **arrow with a bar across** = it must jump over one piece;
- a **hollow dot** = a diagonal step allowed only along the lines of a palace.

| Piece | How it moves |
|---|---|
| General 楚 / 漢 (biggest piece) | One step along the lines of the palace, diagonals included. Never leaves the palace. |
| Guard 士 (small) | Same as the general: one step along the palace lines, inside the palace. |
| Elephant 象 | One point straight, then two diagonally outward. Blocked if any point on the way is taken. |
| Horse 馬 | One point straight, then one diagonally outward. Blocked if the first point is taken. |
| Chariot 車 | Any distance along a line, and along the diagonal lines inside a palace. |
| Cannon 包 | Must jump over **exactly one** piece to move **and** to capture. It can never jump over another cannon, nor capture one. Inside a palace it can also jump along the diagonals. |
| Soldier 卒 / 兵 (small) | One step forward or sideways, never backward. Inside the enemy palace it may also step diagonally forward along the lines. |

Tap **Pieces** at any time to see the list with big pictures.

## Special rules

- **Check** and **checkmate** work as in chess: checkmate wins.
- **Passing**: instead of moving you may tap **Pass**, but not while you are in check. If none of your pieces can move, you must pass.
- **Generals facing each other** (bikjang): if the two generals stand on the same line with nothing between them, the game ends as a **draw** in this app (a simple version of the traditional rule).
- If both players pass one after the other, the game is a draw.

## Playing in the app

1. Tap one of your blue pieces: green dots show where it may go.
2. Tap a green dot to move. Captured pieces are shown above and below the board.
3. **Undo** takes back your last move and the computer's answer; **Hint** shows a good move.

## Levels

- **Easy**: looks one move ahead and often blunders.
- **Medium**: looks a few moves ahead.
- **Hard**: thinks as deeply as it can in under a second. It only passes when it has no other move.

## What is simplified

Everyone uses the same starting setup (horse, elephant, elephant, horse from the left on both sides); the traditional choice of swapping horses and elephants is not offered. There is no point count at the end. The game is a draw when the same position comes back **three times**, after **60 moves each without a capture**, or when neither side has an attacking piece left.

## Tips

- The chariot is the strongest piece, then the cannon and the horse.
- A cannon needs another piece to jump over: early in the game, open a "screen" for it.
- Use the palace diagonals: a chariot or cannon on a palace corner attacks the opposite corner.
