# Five in a row (Gomoku)

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/gomoku.md) · [Español](../../es/games/gomoku.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/gomoku.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/gomoku.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/gomoku.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/gomoku.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/gomoku.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/gomoku.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/gomoku.md)

A game for two on the crossings of a 15×15 board. You play black and go first; the computer plays white. The first to make an unbroken line of five or more stones, across, down or diagonally, wins.

## How to play

- Tap a crossing: a faint stone shows where it will go. Tap the same crossing again to place it, or another crossing to change your mind.
- The last stone played has a small ring, and the winning line is highlighted.
- **Undo** takes back your last move (and the computer's answer). **Hint** marks a good crossing.

## Rules

- Free style: five or more in a row wins, six or more included.
- The tournament restrictions on Black (no "double three", no "double four", no line longer than five) are **not** used, so Black, who starts, has an advantage.
- If the board fills up, the game is a draw.

## Levels

- **Easy**: picks the most promising crossing, but sometimes a worse one on purpose, and may even forget to block a four.
- **Medium**: always completes its own fours and blocks yours, and looks a few moves ahead.
- **Hard**: looks deeper, and searches for a win by a series of fours, all in under a second.

## Tips

- An **open three** (three in a row with both ends free) must be blocked at once, or it becomes an open four that can no longer be stopped.
- Two threats at the same time win: try to make a four and an open three with one stone.
