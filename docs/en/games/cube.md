# Magic cube

The color cube puzzle: a cube made of small cubes whose layers turn. Mix it up, then turn the layers until every face shows a single color again. There is no timer and no hurry.

## How to play

- **Turn a layer:** swipe across a sticker. The row or column under your finger moves the way your finger goes.
- **Big buttons:** if swiping is awkward, pick a face in the row of small face pictures (each one shows that face as it is now; the chosen face is outlined on the cube), then tap ↺ (anticlockwise) or ↻ (clockwise). On the 4×4 and 5×5, the **Layer** button picks the layer behind the face (Layer 1 is the face itself).
- **Look around:** drag on the empty space around the cube, or use the four arrows to turn the whole cube a quarter turn. Turning the whole cube never counts as a move.
- **Undo** takes back the last turn. **Hint** makes the next turn for you (see below).
- The cube is solved when every face has one color. The game saves itself after every turn.

## Sizes

| Size | What it is like |
|---|---|
| **2×2×2** | 8 pieces, only corners. The gentlest start. |
| **3×3×3** | The classic cube. |
| **4×4×4** | No fixed centers: the center pieces move too. |
| **5×5×5** | The biggest, for patient hands. |

## Levels

The level says how mixed up the cube is at the start:

- **Easy:** only a few random turns (3 to 5). You can often solve it by undoing them in your head.
- **Medium:** more turns (6 on the 2×2 up to 18 on the 5×5).
- **Hard:** fully mixed.

## The hint

- **2×2 and 3×3:** the hint follows the beginner method below, one turn at a time, and the line at the top tells you which step it is working on ("Step 2 of 7: bottom corners"). It builds the first layer on the face that is at the bottom. If you keep pressing Hint, the cube gets solved; if you make turns of your own in between, it simply plans again from there.
- **4×4 and 5×5:** these have no short beginner method, so the hint walks back the way the cube came: first your own turns, then the turns that mixed it, one at a time. The line at the top shows how many turns are left.

## A beginner's method for the 3×3

Notation: hold the cube with one face towards you. **R** means turn the right face clockwise (as if you were looking straight at that face), **L** the left face, **U** the top (up), **D** the bottom (down), **F** the front. A **'** means anticlockwise, and **2** means twice. In the app, choose the face with the face buttons and use ↻ for a letter alone and ↺ for a letter with '.

1. **Bottom cross.** Choose a color for the bottom. Bring the four edge pieces with that color around the bottom center, each one also matching the center of its side. Take your time: this step is done by eye.
2. **Bottom corners.** Find a corner with the bottom color in the top layer and turn the top until it is above its place (between the right colors). Repeat **R U R' U'** until it drops into place the right way round.
3. **Middle layer.** Find an edge in the top layer without the top color. Turn the top until its front color matches the front center. If it belongs on the right: **U R U' R' U' F' U F**. On the left: **U' L' U L U F U' F'**.
4. **Top cross.** Repeat **F R U R' U' F'** until the top color makes a cross (dot → L shape → line → cross; hold the L at the back left and the line from left to right).
5. **Top edges.** Turn the top so that two edges match their sides, put them at the back and the right, and do **R U R' U R U2 R' U**. Repeat until all four match.
6. **Top corners into place.** With a corner that is already in its place (any way round) at the front right, do **U R U' L' U R' U' L** until all four corners are in their places.
7. **Turn the top corners.** Hold the cube with an unfinished corner at the front right of the top. Repeat **R' D' R D** until that corner shows the top color on top. Then turn only the top (**U**) to bring the next unfinished corner there and repeat. The bottom looks broken in the meantime: it comes back by itself at the end.

## The 2×2

It works like the corners of the 3×3: (1) build the bottom layer as in step 2, (2) make the top one color with **R U R' U R U2 R'** (repeat, turning the top in between), (3) put the top corners in place with **R U R' U' R' F R2 U' R' U' R U R' F'**, which swaps the two corners on the right.

## Tips

- Solve one layer at a time and do not worry if a finished part breaks for a moment inside an algorithm: it comes back at the end.
- In the color-blind palette every color also has its own symbol: dot, star, heart, square, triangle and ring.
- Undo has no limit: feel free to try things.
