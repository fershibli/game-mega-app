# Hearts

Hearts ("Copas" in Brazil) is the classic game where you try **not** to win points: avoid the hearts and above all the queen of spades. Four players, each for themselves: **you against three computer players**, or four people **taking turns on one phone**. In French it is also known as *la dame de pique*.

## The cards
- The full 52-card deck, **13 cards each**. Aces are high.
- Each **heart** you take is **1 point**; the **queen of spades** is **13 points**. 26 points in all.

## Passing
Before each hand everybody chooses **3 cards** to pass: to the **left**, then to the **right**, then **across**, then nobody passes, and again. Tap three cards (they rise) and press **Pass 3 cards**. The cards you receive are ringed until the first trick is over.

## Playing
- The holder of the **2♣** starts the first trick with it. Play goes to the left.
- **Follow suit** when you can; otherwise play any card. On the first trick nobody may play a heart or the queen of spades unless they have nothing else.
- Hearts may not be led until a heart has been played (unless you have only hearts).
- The strongest card of the suit led takes the trick — there are no trumps. The winner starts the next trick.
- Tap a card (it rises) and tap it again, or press **Play card**. Greyed cards cannot be played now; tapping one tells you why. **Hint** suggests a card (and three cards to pass). There is no Undo: everyone has seen your card.

## Shooting the moon
If you take **all the hearts and the queen of spades**, you score 0 and each of the others gets **26 points**.

## End of the game
When somebody reaches **100 points** at the end of a hand, the game ends and the **lowest score wins**.

## Levels
The computer never looks at hidden cards. It knows its own cards, the cards played, the cards it passed and which suits someone has shown they lack.
- **Easy:** passes three random cards and often plays a random card.
- **Medium:** passes the queen of spades, high spades and high hearts, plays under the winning card when it can, drops the queen and high hearts on others when it lacks the suit, and leads small spades to drive out the queen.
- **Hard:** also imagines many deals of the unseen cards and plays each card to the end of the hand, choosing the one that costs it the fewest points.

## Tips
- Get rid of a short suit early: when you lack a suit you can throw away dangerous cards.
- Keep small spades to protect yourself when spades are led and the queen is still out.
