# Word search

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/wordsearch.md) · [Español](../../es/games/wordsearch.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/wordsearch.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/wordsearch.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/wordsearch.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/wordsearch.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/wordsearch.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/wordsearch.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/wordsearch.md)

The words of a list are hidden in a grid of letters. Find them all!

## How to play

- Slide your finger from the first letter of a word to its last letter. The line follows your finger in straight lines only: across, down or diagonally.
- If sliding is difficult, tap the first letter and then the last one.
- A word you find gets a colored band, and it is crossed out in the list below the grid. Every band also has its own pattern (plain, with edges, with dashed edges or hollow), so the words stay apart even without telling colors apart.
- Tap a word of the list to see its clue.
- **Hint** circles the first letter of a word you have not found yet; tap it again to circle its last letter too.
- The words of a grid come from the same corner of the dictionary, so they often share a topic: food, the home, animals...

## Levels

- **Easy**: 8×8 grid, 6 words, which read only left to right or top to bottom.
- **Medium**: 10×10 grid, 8 words, also on the diagonals.
- **Hard**: 12×12 grid, 10 words, in every direction, backwards too.

## Languages

The words come from the crossword dictionary of the language you use. In Arabic the grid reads from right to left; in Japanese the words are written in katakana, one kana per square; in Chinese each square holds one character.

## Tips

- Look for an unusual letter of the word (a K, a Z...) and check its neighbours.
- On Hard, remember that words can also run backwards.
