# Parking lot

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/parking.md) · [Español](../../es/games/parking.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/parking.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/parking.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/parking.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/parking.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/parking.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/parking.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/parking.md)

A full car park on a 6 × 6 grid. Get the **red car** out through the gap in the wall on the right of its row.

## How to play
- **Drag** a car (2 squares) or a truck (3 squares) with your finger. Each one only moves along its own direction, forwards or backwards, as far as the way is clear; it never turns and never passes through another.
- Every slide counts as one move, however far it goes.
- When nothing stands between the red car and the gap, slide it out.
- **Undo** takes back a move, **Restart** puts every vehicle back where it started, and **Hint** rings a vehicle and shows with a dashed outline where it should go next on a shortest way out.

Every puzzle is new, and the app works out the fewest moves it needs; that number is shown next to yours.

## Levels
| Level | Fewest moves | Vehicles |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 3 to 7 | about 6 to 8 |
| Medium | 8 to 14 | about 9 to 13 |
| Hard | 15 or more | about 9 to 13 |
