# Mancala

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/mancala.md) · [Español](../../es/games/mancala.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/mancala.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/mancala.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/mancala.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/mancala.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/mancala.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/mancala.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/mancala.md)

A family of very old “sowing” games from Africa and the Middle East, for two: you play the **bottom row** of six pits, the computer plays the top row. Your store is the big hole on the right, the computer’s on the left. The big number next to each pit tells how many seeds it holds.

## How to play

- Tap one of your pits that has seeds (they are circled). All its seeds are **sown** one by one into the following holes, counterclockwise: along your row to the right, then along the computer’s row to the left.
- **Undo** takes back your last move (and the computer’s answer). **Hint** circles a good pit.
- At the end, whoever has more seeds wins.

## The rules you can choose

**Kalah**

- 6 pits with 4 seeds each, and each player has a store in the round. You sow into your own store but skip the computer’s.
- If your last seed falls into **your store**, you play again.
- If your last seed falls into **an empty pit of yours** and the pit opposite has seeds, you take both into your store.
- When one side has no seeds left, the game ends and the other player keeps the seeds on their side.

**Oware** (also called awalé)

- 6 pits with 4 seeds each; the stores only keep captured seeds and are not sown into. A pit with 12 or more seeds skips itself when sowing round.
- If your last seed makes **2 or 3** in a pit of the computer, you take them, and also the pits just before it on the computer’s side that now hold 2 or 3.
- A move that would take **every** seed of the computer is allowed but takes nothing.
- If the computer has no seeds, you must play a pit that gives it some. If you cannot, the game ends and you keep your seeds.
- The first to have more than 24 seeds wins. If 100 moves go by without a capture, each player keeps the seeds on their side.

## Levels

- **Easy**: looks one move ahead and now and then plays at random.
- **Medium**: looks about four moves ahead.
- **Hard**: thinks as far as it can in under a second.

## Tips

- In Kalah, count the seeds: a pit with exactly the right number to reach your store gives a free turn. Play those first, from the pit closest to the store.
- In Oware, avoid leaving pits with 1 or 2 seeds where the computer can reach them.
