# Mahjong

**Goal:** remove all tiles in matching pairs.

A tile is **free** when no tile lies on top of it and its left **or** right side is open. Tap two free, identical tiles to remove them. Any two **flowers** match each other, and any two **seasons** match each other. Each suit tile shows a small Western number (or E/S/W/N for winds) in its corner, so you do not need to read Chinese.

On Easy and Medium, tiles that are **not free are dimmed**. **Hint** outlines a pair, **Undo** puts the last pair back, **Shuffle** re-deals the remaining tiles when you are stuck.

| Level | Tiles | Shuffles |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 48 (tower) | unlimited |
| Medium | 96 (fortress) | 3 |
| Hard | 144 (classic turtle) | 1 |

Every deal is built so that it **can always be solved**.
