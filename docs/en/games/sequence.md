# Follow the colors

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/sequence.md) · [Español](../../es/games/sequence.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/sequence.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/sequence.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/sequence.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/sequence.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/sequence.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/sequence.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/sequence.md)

A memory game for the eyes: four big squares light up one after another, and you repeat the order.

## How to play
Tap **Start** when you are ready. The game shows the order (**Watch…**) by lighting the squares, one at a time. Then it says **Your turn**: tap the squares in the same order. Each round adds one more color at the end. Repeat the whole order up to the goal of your level to win.

Each square has its own color **and its own symbol** (star, drop, leaf, heart), so you can follow the symbols if colors are hard to tell apart. The app makes no sound; the phone may give a light buzz when you tap.

Your longest sequence is kept for each level.

## Levels
| Level | Goal | Pace | A mistake… |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 5 colors | slow | shows the same colors again, and **Watch again** is always there |
| Medium | 8 colors | calm | ends the game |
| Hard | 12 colors | a bit faster | ends the game |

## Tips
- Name the symbols as they light up: "star, leaf, star, heart".
- Group them in twos or threes, like a phone number.
