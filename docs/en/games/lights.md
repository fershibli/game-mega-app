# Switch off the lights

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/lights.md) · [Español](../../es/games/lights.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/lights.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/lights.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/lights.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/lights.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/lights.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/lights.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/lights.md)

A grid of lamps, some of them lit. Tapping a lamp switches it **and its neighbours above, below and to the sides**: lit lamps go dark and dark ones light up. Switch every lamp off.

## How to play
- Tap a lamp to switch it and its neighbours.
- **Hint** puts a ring on a lamp to tap. **Restart** brings back the starting lamps.
- Every puzzle can be solved, and there is no clock.

## Levels
- **Easy:** 3 × 3 lamps, solved in a few taps.
- **Medium:** 5 × 5 lamps.
- **Hard:** 6 × 6 lamps, with more of them lit.

## Tips
- Tapping the same lamp twice undoes it, and the order of the taps doesn't matter.
- Go row by row: to switch off a lit lamp, tap the lamp just below it.
