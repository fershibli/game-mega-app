# Pet friend

A little friend lives in a cozy room on your phone. Look after it whenever you like: there is no hurry, no timer, and nothing bad ever happens.

## Your first visit

Choose one of ten friends: cat, dog, bunny, chick, panda, frog, fox, penguin, hamster or turtle. Then tap one of the suggested names (or type your own) and tap **Adopt**.

## The four bars

**Happiness**, **Food**, **Energy** and **Clean** go down very slowly, about a day from full to empty, even while the app is closed. They never go below 20 %: that pale bit at the start of a bar is as empty as it gets. Your friend never gets sick, never runs away and never scolds you. When a bar is low it just looks a little sleepy or hungry, shows a small thought bubble, and is happy to see you.

## Things to do

- **Feed**: an apple, a carrot, a fish or a cookie. Every friend has a favorite.
- **Cuddle**: stroke your friend with your finger and little hearts appear.
- **Kiss** and **Wave**: your friend kisses back and waves hello.
- **Bath**: lots of bubbles, and it is nice and clean.
- **Sleep**: the lights go down and energy comes back. Tap **Wake up** when you like.
- **Walk**: a stroll in the garden.
- **Play**: rock-paper-scissors, tic-tac-toe (your marks are hearts, your friend's are paw prints; it plays simply and sometimes lets you win) or the ball.
- **Dress up**: a bow, a hat, a flower or a scarf.

## Friends and adopt

You can adopt another kind of friend at any time, after a confirmation. The friend who was visiting waits in the garden, and you can call it back from the same screen: nobody is ever lost.

## Saving

Everything is saved on the phone straight away. Closing the app, restarting the phone, playing another game or **New game** in the settings never erases your friend. Uninstalling the app does.

## Tips

- There is nothing to win or lose: just visit when you feel like it.
- If you do not like dragging, the **Cuddle** button gives a cuddle too.
