# Color drops

A pair of round colored drops falls slowly down a narrow well (6 columns, 12 rows). Line up drops of the same color: when **3 or more drops of one color touch** (up, down, left or right), they pop and the drops above them fall. If that fall makes another group pop, it is a **chain**, worth more points.

Every color also has its own symbol (dot, square, triangle, diamond, cross), so you never need to tell colors apart by hue alone.

## How to play
- Press **Start** when you are ready. Nothing moves before that.
- The big buttons move the pair **left** and **right**, **turn** it, or **drop** it straight down. You can also swipe on the well: sideways to move, down to drop, a tap to turn.
- **Pause** freezes everything. The game also pauses by itself when you open the settings or leave the app, and waits for **Continue**.
- The game ends when the top of the middle column (where new pairs enter) is full. Your score and your best score for that level are shown.

## Points
- 10 points for each drop that pops.
- In a chain, every new pop is worth double the one before (up to ×16).

## Levels
- **Easy:** 3 colors, a very slow fall, the next pair is shown, and the pair waits while your finger rests on the buttons.
- **Medium:** 4 colors and a slow fall.
- **Hard:** 5 colors and a slightly faster fall (still calm).

## Tips
- Keep the middle column low, so new pairs always have room to enter.
- Stack two drops of a color and keep a third nearby: dropping it later starts a chain.
