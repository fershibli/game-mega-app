# Killer sudoku

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/killer.md) · [Español](../../es/games/killer.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/killer.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/killer.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/killer.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/killer.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/killer.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/killer.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/killer.md)

A sudoku with no numbers given. As usual, each row, column and 3×3 box holds the digits 1 to 9 once. The grid is also divided into cages with dashed outlines: the digits in a cage add up to the small number in its corner, and a cage never repeats a digit.

## How to play

- As in Sudoku: tap a square, then a number; **Notes** for small reminders; **Undo** to take back.
- The squares of the selected cage are tinted.
- **Hint** first shows the combinations that can make the sum of the selected square's cage; tap it again to fill in the square.
- Pinch to zoom in.

## Levels

- **Easy**: about ten cages of a single square (they work like given numbers) and small cages; simple reasoning is enough. Mistakes are shown in red.
- **Medium**: a few single squares and cages of up to 4 or 5 squares; you may need "locked" digits (a digit that must be in one line of a box, or in one part of a cage). Mistakes are shown in red.
- **Hard**: no single squares and bigger cages; you may need the 45 rule. Wrong digits are not shown, but repeats and wrong cage sums still are.

Every puzzle is made for this app and has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone, without guessing.

## Tips

- A cage of one square gives its digit straight away.
- Two squares adding up to 3 hold 1 and 2; 17 is 8 and 9; three squares adding up to 6 hold 1, 2 and 3.
- The 45 rule: each row, column and box adds up to 45. If the cages inside a box cover all of it but one square, that square is 45 minus their total.
