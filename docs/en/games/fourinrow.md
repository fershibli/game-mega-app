# Four in a row

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/fourinrow.md) · [Español](../../es/games/fourinrow.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/fourinrow.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/fourinrow.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/fourinrow.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/fourinrow.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/fourinrow.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/fourinrow.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/fourinrow.md)

A game for two on an upright wooden stand with 7 columns and 6 rows of holes. You play the light discs and go first; the computer plays the dark ones. The first to line up four discs, across, up and down or diagonally, wins.

**Take turns on this phone:** two people can also play against each other, without the computer. The board never turns around and the screen says whose turn it is.

## How to play

- Tap anywhere in a column, from the top of the board to the bottom: your disc drops into the lowest free hole.
- A full column takes no more discs. If all 42 holes fill up without four in a row, the game is a draw.
- The winning line is highlighted.
- **Undo** takes back your last move (and the computer's answer). **Hint** tints a good column.

## Levels

- **Easy**: always takes a win, usually blocks yours, and otherwise looks only two moves ahead, sometimes dropping a disc anywhere.
- **Medium**: looks six moves ahead.
- **Hard**: looks as far ahead as it can in about two thirds of a second. Very hard to beat.

## Tips

- The middle column is the most valuable: more lines of four pass through it.
- Before dropping a disc, check that it does not let the computer win by playing right on top of it.
- Prepare two threats at once, for example one just above the other: the computer can only block one.
