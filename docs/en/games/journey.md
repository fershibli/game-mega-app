# Journey

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/journey.md) · [Español](../../es/games/journey.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/journey.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/journey.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/journey.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/journey.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/journey.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/journey.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/journey.md)

A calm adventure in turns, in a kingdom of castles and knights with friends from Brazilian folklore. Nobody hurries you: enemies wait for your move, and losing costs nothing.

## Starting out
Choose a **class** and type a **name**:

| Class | Good at |
|---|---|
| Knight | Lots of life and a **Strong blow**. |
| Archer | Quick; **Arrow rain** reaches every enemy. |
| Mage | **Fire** and **Ice** magic. |
| Healer | Heals the group with **Light**. |

**Bento** the blacksmith comes along from the start: he can **Protect** the group and take the blows. In the forest you can rescue **Lia**, a fire mage.

## The map and the village
Tap a spot on the map to go there. A spot opens when the one before it is done; battles already won can be played again to grow stronger. Chests and friends give potions, ethers and gold. In the **village** you can **rest at the inn** (everyone back to full life and mana), ask **Saci** for tips and change how strong the enemies are (Easy, Medium, Hard) at any time.

## Battles
The strip at the top shows who plays next: the fastest go first. On your turn choose:
- **Attack**, then whom.
- **Magic**: each spell costs mana.
- **Defend**: half damage until your next turn, and a little mana back.
- **Item**: a potion gives back half the life; an ether gives back mana.
- **Best move** plays a good move for you, and **Automatic** plays every turn of the group.

Each enemy is **weak** to a kind of magic (one and a half times the damage) and **resists** another (half). After the first hit, a big icon shows its weak spot. Enemies never die: they run away or faint.

| Enemy | Weak to | Resists |
|---|---|---|
| Green slime | Fire | Ice |
| Boar | Ice | Fire |
| Sleepy mushroom | Fire | Light |
| Grey wolf | Light | Ice |
| Giant boar (boss) | Ice | Fire |

If the whole group faints, it wakes up rested at the inn, with everything it had.

## Levels
Battles give experience and gold. At each new level you choose an extra: more life, attack, defense, mana or speed, or a new spell.

## Five regions
The map has arrows to move between regions. Each one opens once the boss of the one before has run away.

| Region | Friend you rescue | Enemies, and what they fear | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forest | Lia (fire mage) | slime (Fire), boar (Ice), mushroom (Fire), wolf (Light) | Giant boar (Ice) |
| River | Iara (heals, shields, soaks) | frog (Ice), crab (Nature), piranha (Light) | Giant crab (Nature) |
| Hills | Zeca (rogue: steals, throws oil) | bat (Light), gnome (Fire), rockling (Nature) | Hill troll (Arcane) |
| Ruins | Tito (puts enemies to sleep) | ghost (Light), empty armor (Arcane), spider (Fire) | Old golem (Arcane) |
| Castle | Dora (bard: courage for all) | imp (Ice), gargoyle (Nature), dark knight (Light) | Shadow King (Light) |

Each region also has a friendly figure with a tip (the Boto, the Caipora, a wise owl, an old guard), and some chests hold a **scroll** that teaches the hero a spell.

## The shop and the tavern
- In the village **shop**: potions, ethers, swords (attack), armor (defense) and charms (mana). Gear you bought stays yours; tap **Wear** to change.
- Up to **three friends** travel with the hero. The others wait at the **tavern**, where you choose who goes. In a battle, **Swap a friend** brings one in from the tavern (it uses the turn).

## More magic
- **Nature**: Thorns (poison: a little life lost each turn), Vines (all enemies, slower), Wave (soaks them).
- **Arcane**: Arcane bolt, Sleep (skips turns until hit; bosses only doze), Barrier (half damage for a while), Song of courage (stronger blows).
- **Combos**: a **soaked** enemy hit by **Ice** freezes (more damage, and it loses a turn); an enemy covered in **oil** hit by **Fire** burns twice as hard.
