# Find the pairs

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/memory.md) · [Español](../../es/games/memory.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/memory.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/memory.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/memory.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/memory.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/memory.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/memory.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/memory.md)

The classic card game of memory: all cards lie face down, and you look for the matching pairs.

## How to play
Tap a card to turn it over, then tap a second one.
- **Same picture:** a pair! Both stay up (slightly faded, with a green frame), and you play again.
- **Different pictures:** both stay up so you can look at them calmly. Tap any card to go on: the two turn back down (and the card you tapped turns up, if it was hidden).

There is no timer. Every two cards turned over count as one **move**; try to find every pair in as few moves as you can. Your best result is kept for each set of pictures and each level.

## The pictures
- **Playing cards** — a pair is the same number in the same color: ♥ with ♦, or ♠ with ♣ (for example the 7 of hearts with the 7 of diamonds).
- **Fruits** — apple, pear, cherries, banana, lemon, strawberry, grapes, watermelon, pineapple, orange, avocado, kiwi, pumpkin, pomegranate and coconut.
- **Symbols** — star, heart, moon, sun, drop, leaf, flower, cloud, lightning, gem, triangle, ring, plus, house and crown.
- **Animals** — cat, dog, rabbit, bear, pig, mouse, owl, frog, chick, fish, panda, fox, lion, cow and sheep.

Every picture has its own shape, not only its own color, so the game works for every kind of color vision.

## Levels
| Level | Cards | Pairs |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 3 × 4 | 6 |
| Medium | 4 × 4 | 8 |
| Hard | 5 × 6 | 15 |

## Tips
- Turn over a card you have never seen first; then you know what to look for.
- Say the pictures quietly to yourself ("cherries top left") — it helps you remember.
