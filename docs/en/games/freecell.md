# Free Cells

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/freecell.md) · [Español](../../es/games/freecell.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/freecell.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/freecell.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/freecell.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/freecell.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/freecell.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/freecell.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/freecell.md)

A patience game where **every card is face up** from the start, so it is all about thinking ahead. Build four piles from **Ace to King, one per suit**. Almost every deal can be won, and in this app every deal can.

## How to play

- The 52 cards are dealt on eight columns. Above them are **four free cells** (on the side of your thumb) and **four foundations**, one per suit.
- On the columns, a card goes on a card **one rank higher of the other color** (a red 6 on a black 7). Any card can go on an empty column.
- Each free cell holds **one card**, to get it out of the way for a while.
- Tap a card: it goes to the best place (home first, then another column, then a free cell, then an empty column). Tap it again to try the next place.
- You can move several cards in order at once if there is room to carry them: (free cells + 1), doubled for each empty column. If there is not enough room, the game tells you how many cards you can move right now.
- When all the columns are in order, **Finish** sends every card home.
- **Undo** takes back moves. **Hint** asks a solver for the next move of a winning line. After every move the game checks whether the deal can still be won; if not, it tells you so and you can undo, give up or keep looking.

## Levels

Every deal is checked by a solver before you see it, so you can always win.

- **Easy**: deals the solver wins using only two of the four free cells. With all four you have plenty of room.
- **Medium**: deals that need three free cells.
- **Hard**: deals that need all four free cells and careful planning.

## Tips

- Free the Aces and Twos first: look for them under other cards.
- Keep free cells empty as long as you can; a full set of cells blocks everything.
- An empty column is worth more than a free cell: it doubles how many cards you can move.
- Before moving, look a few moves ahead: where will the card you uncover go?
