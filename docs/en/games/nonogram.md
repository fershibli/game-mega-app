# Nonogram

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/nonogram.md) · [Español](../../es/games/nonogram.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/nonogram.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/nonogram.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/nonogram.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/nonogram.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/nonogram.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/nonogram.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/nonogram.md)

Paint by numbers. The numbers beside each row and above each column give the lengths of the painted blocks in that line, in order; between two blocks there is at least one empty square. Paint the right squares and a picture appears: when you finish, it is shown in color with its name.

## How to play

- Tap a square to paint it; tap it again to clear it.
- Hold a square to mark it with ✕ (a square you know stays empty). With **Mark** turned on, a tap marks ✕ and holding paints.
- Drag along a row or a column to paint (or mark) several squares at once.
- The numbers of a line turn pale when its painted squares match them.
- **Undo** takes back the last change. **Hint** first corrects a mistake; otherwise it paints a square that one line already decides.
- Pinch to zoom in on the bigger grids.

## Levels

- **Easy**: 5×5. Mistakes are shown in red.
- **Medium**: 10×10. Mistakes are shown in red.
- **Hard**: 15×15. Mistakes are not shown.

Every puzzle can be solved one line at a time, without guessing. The pictures (more than 20 for each size: animals, flowers, a house, a boat, a teapot...) were drawn for this app; if none fits, a random pattern is used.

## Tips

- Start with the big numbers: a block of 8 in a line of 10 always covers the 6 middle squares.
- A line with 0 is entirely empty: mark it with ✕.
- Mark the empty squares with ✕: they help the lines that cross them.
