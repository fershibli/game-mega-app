# Anagrams

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/anagram.md) · [Español](../../es/games/anagram.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/anagram.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/anagram.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/anagram.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/anagram.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/anagram.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/anagram.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/anagram.md)

The letters of a word have been mixed up. Put them back in order!

## How to play

- Tap the big letter tiles in the right order: each one goes to the next empty place of the answer.
- Tapping a letter of the answer sends it back. **Undo** takes back the last letter. **Shuffle** mixes the tiles again, which sometimes helps you see the word.
- When the word is right it lights up and the next word comes. A round has five words.
- **Hint** places the next right letter (and takes back the wrong ones first). You can always finish a word with hints, so nobody gets stuck.
- When the letters also spell another word of the dictionary, that word is accepted too.

## Levels

- **Easy**: short words (4–5 letters); the clue is shown and the first letter is already in place.
- **Medium**: 6–7 letters, with the clue.
- **Hard**: 8 letters or more, without the clue: the first **Hint** shows it.

Arabic and Japanese words are shorter (3, 4 and 5–6 letters or kana) and Chinese ones are counted in characters (3, 3–4 and 4).

## Tips

- Read the clue first, then look for common endings (-ING, -ER...).
- Put the letters you are sure of first and try the others afterwards.
