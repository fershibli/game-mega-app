# Farm

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/farm.md) · [Español](../../es/games/farm.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/farm.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/farm.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/farm.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/farm.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/farm.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/farm.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/farm.md)

A small farm that lives on your phone. Sow, water and harvest, look after hens, a cow and sheep, sell in the barn and fill the neighbours' orders. There is no hurry and nothing is ever lost: crops never wilt, animals never go hungry and the farm keeps growing while the app is closed.

Three buttons at the top open the **Farm**, the **Orders** and the **Barn**. Above them, a gold **coin** and a **star** show how many coins and stars you have. The farm is one picture: the field of beds, the paddock with the animals, the house and the barn. Pinch to see it bigger, and the round button brings it back. When zoomed in, drag with one finger to move around.

When you tap a bed or an animal, what it says (its name, the time left, "Harvested!") appears on a small card over the top of the farm. It goes away by itself after a few seconds, or when you tap it, and it never moves the buttons.

## The field
- Tap an **empty bed** (bare brown soil) and choose what to sow. Each seed costs a few coins. The price shows next to a small coin picture, like every price on the farm.
- A crop grows by itself in real time: first a sprout, then a young plant with a small bar that fills up, then the ripe crop, which sparkles. Tap a growing bed to read its name and the time left.
- **Watering** (tap a bed with a blue drop, or **Water all**) makes the rest of the growing go twice as fast. Once per crop is enough.
- Tap a ripe bed: it gives **two** of its crop, which go straight into the barn. **Collect all** does it for every ripe bed at once, and also takes what the animals have made. It lights up when something is ready, and says what went into the barn, such as "In the barn: Lettuce ×4, Egg ×2".
- **New bed** adds a bed to the field, up to twelve. It asks first, with a picture of a bed growing, and needs something already sown: that way there is always a harvest to sell. The new bed blinks for a moment so you can see where it went.

## The animals
Buy animals with **Buy an animal**. Each one makes its product on its own and keeps up to **three** waiting, shown in a little bubble above it; tap the animal (or **Collect all**, which also harvests the ripe beds) to take them to the barn.

| Animal | Gives | Every | Costs | Sells for | Stars needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken | Egg | 10 min | 40 | 10 | 0 |
| Cow | Milk | 30 min | 120 | 22 | 2 |
| Sheep | Wool | 60 min | 200 | 40 | 4 |

## Building and the mill
The **Build** button (next to **New bed** and **Buy an animal**) opens a list of what can be built, each with its picture, its price next to the coin and how many stars are still missing. What is not available yet shows faded; tap one that is ready to build it. The button disappears once everything is built.

The **mill** needs **6 stars** and costs **120** coins. It stands in the meadow at the back left of the farm, beside the field. Its sails turn slowly while it works and stop when it rests (they stay still if animations are off). Once it is built, wheat joins the seeds and the neighbours also start asking for corn meal and flour.

Tap the mill to open its card: each recipe shows its ingredients and how many the barn has. **Grind** takes the ingredients from the barn and starts, and a bar shows the time. The mill grinds one recipe at a time and keeps up to **3** in its queue. If something is missing, the button stays in view and says what ("Missing: Corn ×2"). When a sack is ready, a bubble appears over the mill; tap the mill or **Collect all** to take it to the barn.

| Product | Ingredients | Time | Sells for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corn meal | 2 Corn | 10 min | 22 |
| Flour | 2 Wheat | 12 min | 26 |

## Orders and stars
Neighbours ask for a few things at a time and pay **half as much again** as the market, plus a **star**. **Deliver** lights up when the barn has everything. **Another order** swaps an order for a new one, free. Stars bring new seeds and animals, as the tables show.

## The barn
Everything you harvest and collect waits in the barn, with its price. **Sell 1** or **Sell all** turns it into coins. If you ever run out of coins, there is always something to sell, or a lettuce to sow for two coins.

## Saving
Everything is saved on the phone straight away. Closing the app, playing another game or **New game** in the settings never erases the farm. Uninstalling the app does.
