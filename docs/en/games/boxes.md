# Box pusher

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/boxes.md) · [Español](../../es/games/boxes.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/boxes.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/boxes.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/boxes.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/boxes.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/boxes.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/boxes.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/boxes.md)

Walk the round friend around the room and push every box onto a round spot.

## How to play
- Swipe, use the arrows, or tap a square to walk there.
- Walk into a box to push it one square. Boxes can only be pushed, never pulled, and only one at a time.
- **Undo** as often as you like; **Restart** starts the room again; **Hint** shows the next step.
- If a box gets stuck where it can no longer reach a spot, the game tells you: tap Undo to free it.

## Levels
- **Easy:** 2 boxes in a small room.
- **Medium:** 3 boxes.
- **Hard:** 4 boxes in a bigger room with more walls.

## Tips
- Look before pushing a box against a wall: from there it can only slide along it.
- A box in a corner can't come out, so keep boxes away from corners without a spot.
