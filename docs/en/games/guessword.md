# Guess the word

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/guessword.md) · [Español](../../es/games/guessword.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/guessword.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/guessword.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/guessword.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/guessword.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/guessword.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/guessword.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/guessword.md)

Guess a hidden word one letter at a time, with a flower to look after: each letter that is not in the word makes one of its petals fall. Find the word before the last petal falls!

## How to play

- The places of the word are shown as empty squares, with a clue below.
- Tap a letter on the keyboard. If it is in the word, it appears in **every** place where it belongs (a letter that appears twice shows twice at once) and its key gets a ✓.
- If it is not in the word, one petal falls gently to the grass and the key gets a —. The letters already tried that are not in the word are listed under the clue.
- When you find the word, the flower opens wide and the sun comes out.
- If the last petal falls, the word is shown, with no fuss, and a new flower is planted for the next word.
- **Hint** shows the clue when it is hidden, then puts the letter of the first empty place. No petal falls for a hint.
- A round has five words. At the end you see how many you found and how many petals you kept.

In Japanese you pick katakana and in Chinese you pick characters from a small bank instead of a keyboard.

## Levels

- **Easy**: 8 petals, the clue and one letter already shown.
- **Medium**: 6 petals and the clue.
- **Hard**: 5 petals, longer words, and the clue only after a **Hint**.

## Tips

- Start with the most common letters: vowels, then letters such as S, T, R, N.
- Read the clue again after each letter you find: it often makes the word pop out.
