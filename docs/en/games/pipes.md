# Pipes

**Goal:** turn the pieces so that water from the round source in the middle reaches **every** piece, with no pipe pointing at a wall or a closed side.

Tap a piece to turn it a quarter turn clockwise. Pieces with water turn blue; end pieces are little tanks that fill up. **Hint** turns one wrong piece into place. **Undo** reverses turns. Grid: 5×5 / 7×7 / 9×9. The puzzle is a random tree of pipes, so there are no loops and a solution always exists.
