# Number merge

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/merge.md) · [Español](../../es/games/merge.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/merge.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/merge.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/merge.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/merge.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/merge.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/merge.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/merge.md)

Number tiles on a board. Slide all of them to one side: two equal numbers that meet join into their sum (2 + 2 makes 4, 4 + 4 makes 8...).

## How to play
- Swipe, or use the arrows, to slide every tile.
- After each move a new 2 (sometimes a 4) appears in an empty square.
- Reach the goal number. Then keep playing for a higher score, or finish there.
- The game ends when no tile can move. There is no clock.

## Levels
- **Easy:** 5 × 5 board, make 256; Undo as often as you like.
- **Medium:** 4 × 4 board, make 512; Undo takes back only the last move.
- **Hard:** 4 × 4 board, make 1024; Undo takes back only the last move.

## Tips
- Keep your biggest number in a corner and slide mostly in two directions.
