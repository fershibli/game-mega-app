# Tangram

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/tangram.md) · [Español](../../es/games/tangram.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/tangram.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/tangram.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/tangram.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/tangram.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/tangram.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/tangram.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/tangram.md)

Seven flat pieces (two big triangles, a medium one, two small ones, a square and a parallelogram) make a picture. Fill the shape with all seven pieces, without overlapping.

## How to play
- Drag a piece to move it, and tap it to turn it.
- **Flip** turns the parallelogram over.
- Pieces snap into place when they are close. **Hint** places one piece; **Start over** takes them all back.

## Levels
- **Easy:** the outline of every piece is drawn inside the shape.
- **Medium:** three of the pieces are drawn.
- **Hard:** only the shape.

## Tips
- Place the two big triangles first: they fill most of the shape.
