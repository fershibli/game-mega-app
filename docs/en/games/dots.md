# Dots

**Goal:** reach the targets shown at the top (e.g. collect 15 red, 15 blue and 15 green dots) before you run out of moves.

Drag across **neighboring dots of the same color** (up, down, left, right; at least two). When you lift your finger they disappear and new dots fall from the top. **Close a square** (come back to a dot already in your line) to clear **every dot of that color** — the board glows when you have a square. Moves turn red when 3 or fewer remain. Easy: 6×6, 4 colors, 22 moves. Medium: 7×7, 5 colors, 20 moves. Hard: 8×8, 5 colors, 18 moves, four targets.
