# Block puzzle

A calm puzzle with no clock and nothing falling. Below the board you get three pieces; put them anywhere they fit. When a whole row or a whole column is full, it clears and gives you points. When the three pieces are used, three new ones arrive.

## How to play
- **Drag** a piece from below onto the board. While you drag, the piece floats a little above your finger so you can always see it; a shadow shows where it will land, and the rows and columns it would complete light up.
- Or **tap** a piece (it gets a frame) and then **tap the board** where you want it.
- **Hint** shows a good place for one of your pieces. **Undo** takes back the last piece.
- The game ends when none of the pieces left fits on the board. Your score and your best score for that level are shown; the best score is kept even when you start other games.

## Points
- 1 point for every block you place.
- Clearing lines: 10 points for one line, 30 for two at once, 60 for three, and so on.

## Levels
- **Easy:** a 9 × 9 board, mostly small pieces, and a **Turn piece** button to rotate the chosen piece.
- **Medium:** a 10 × 10 board and a mix of small and large pieces.
- **Hard:** a 10 × 10 board with more large pieces (lines of five, big corners, crosses).

## Tips
- Keep the middle of the board open: large pieces need room.
- Try to clear a row and a column with the same piece for extra points.
- Before placing a piece, check that the other two will still have a place.
