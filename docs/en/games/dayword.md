# Word of the day

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/dayword.md) · [Español](../../es/games/dayword.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/dayword.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/dayword.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/dayword.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/dayword.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/dayword.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/dayword.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/dayword.md)

Guess the hidden word. Each day brings a new word on each level, the same for everyone who plays that day in that language.

## How to play

- Write a word on the highlighted line with the keyboard and tap **Check**. Any letters are allowed.
- Each letter then gets a mark below it:
  - **●** the letter is in the word, in this very place (it is shown on a soft band);
  - **○** the letter is in the word, but in another place (it is circled);
  - **—** the letter is not in the word (it fades).
- A letter guessed twice when the word has it only once is marked only once.
- The keyboard shows the same marks, and the **You know** line at the top collects the letters already in place.
- **Hint** first shows a clue, then places one letter in the **You know** line.
- After today's word, a practice word follows. **Another** gives a new practice word whenever you like.

## Levels

- **Easy**: 4–5 letters, 8 lines.
- **Medium**: 5 letters, 7 lines.
- **Hard**: 6 letters, 6 lines.

In Arabic, where short vowels are not written, the words are one letter shorter (3–4, 4 and 5 letters). In Japanese and Chinese you pick kana (3, 4 and 5) or characters (2, 3 and 4: four-character sayings on Hard) from a small bank instead of a keyboard.

## Tips

- Start with a word that has several common letters.
- Use the ● letters where they are, and move the ○ letters to another place.
