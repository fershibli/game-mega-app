# Kakuro

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/kakuro.md) · [Español](../../es/games/kakuro.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/kakuro.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/kakuro.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/kakuro.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/kakuro.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/kakuro.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/kakuro.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/kakuro.md)

A crossword of numbers. Fill each white square with a digit from 1 to 9. Each run of white squares, across or down, adds up to the number in the black square before it: the number in the upper right of a black square is for the run to its right, the number in the lower left for the run below it. A run never repeats a digit.

## How to play

- Tap a white square, then a number. Tap the same number again, or the eraser, to clear it.
- **Notes** turns the numbers into small reminders inside the square.
- The runs through the selected square are tinted and their sums highlighted.
- **Hint** first shows the combinations of digits that can make the sums of the selected square (→ across, ↓ down); tap it again to fill in the square.
- **Undo** takes back the last change. Pinch to zoom in.

## Levels

- **Easy**: 5×5 squares inside the frame, runs of up to 4 squares. Mistakes are shown in red.
- **Medium**: 7×7, runs of up to 5. Mistakes are shown in red.
- **Hard**: 9×9, runs of up to 5. Wrong digits are not shown, but repeated digits and wrong sums still are.

Every puzzle is made for this app and has exactly one solution, found by reasoning alone, without guessing.

## Tips

- Some sums can be made in only one way: in two squares, 3 is 1+2, 4 is 1+3, 16 is 7+9 and 17 is 8+9; in three squares, 6 is 1+2+3 and 24 is 7+8+9.
- Where two runs cross, the digit must fit both of them.
