# Life trail

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/trail.md) · [Español](../../es/games/trail.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/trail.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/trail.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/trail.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/trail.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/trail.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/trail.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/trail.md)

A calm board game for two to four. Roll the die and walk a whole life, from childhood at the bottom of the board to the quiet years at the top. Along the way you collect **hearts**: whoever has the most hearts when everyone has arrived wins. Nobody is ever knocked back to the start and nothing bad happens: this is a stroll, not a race.

Play against the computer (it plays the other pawns) or **take turns on this phone**. The app asks how many sit at the table.

## The board
Six landscapes, one for each part of life (a playground and a school, the city and the university, offices and a workshop, houses and gardens, a port and mountains, a porch and an orchard): **Childhood**, **Youth**, **Work**, **Family**, **Travels** and **The quiet years**. The path snakes up from the flag to the little house.

| Square | What happens |
|---|---|
| ♥ Heart | One heart. |
| 📖 Book | A story of that part of life: read it and tap **Go on**. Most give 1 to 3 hearts; a few move you a square or two. |
| Signpost | A crossroads: **Stay** for 2 hearts, or **Move on** 3 squares. |
| Plain | Nothing; enjoy the view. |

When a pawn stops on a heart, a book, a signpost or the house, its card opens over the board, a little above the bottom. Read it calmly: nothing goes on until you tap **Go on** (at a crossroads, until you tap **Stay** or **Move on**). The computer's cards wait for **Go on** too, and at a crossroads they show which way it chose. Pawns walk one square at a time; the **Waiting time** and **Animations** settings make them slower or quicker.

## Arriving
The first to reach the house gets **5** hearts, the second **3**, the third **2** and the fourth **1**. You don't need an exact roll. The game ends when every pawn has arrived; the score of hearts is shown at the end.

## Tips
- Staying at a crossroads is worth 2 hearts; arriving one place earlier is worth up to 2 more. Near the end, if others are close behind, moving on can pay off.
- On **Easy** the computer decides at random at a crossroads; on **Medium** and **Hard** it races only when that can win a better arrival bonus.
