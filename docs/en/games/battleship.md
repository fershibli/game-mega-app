# Battleship

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/battleship.md) · [Español](../../es/games/battleship.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/battleship.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/battleship.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/battleship.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/battleship.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/battleship.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/battleship.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/battleship.md)

The classic game of hidden fleets, against the computer or taking turns on one phone.

## Before the first shot
Your fleet is arranged for you: straight ships that never touch each other, not even at a corner. Tap **Arrange again** as often as you like, then **Start**. Taking turns, player 2 then arranges theirs while player 1 looks away.

## Playing
- The big sea at the top is the other side's: tap a square to fire. An **ink circle** is water; a **red cross** with a little flame is a hit. Squares are named like on the paper game: letters A–J across the top, numbers 1–10 down the side. A ship hit on every square sinks and appears on the sea.
- The small sea below is yours, with your ships and where the other side has fired.
- One shot each, in turn, as on paper. The last shot is framed.
- Sink the whole other fleet first to win.

| Level | Sea | Fleet | Computer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 8 × 8 | 4 ships (4, 3, 2, 2 squares) | fires at random |
| Medium | 10 × 10 | 5 ships (5, 4, 3, 3, 2) | finishes a ship it has hit |
| Hard | 10 × 10 | 5 ships | also aims where the ships left fit best |

The computer never looks at your fleet.

## Taking turns
After each shot a cover hides the screen and asks to pass the phone; the next player taps the big button to see their own sea.
