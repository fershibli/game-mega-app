# Peg solitaire

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/pegs.md) · [Español](../../es/games/pegs.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/pegs.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/pegs.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/pegs.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/pegs.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/pegs.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/pegs.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/pegs.md)

A calm puzzle for one player, played for centuries on a wooden board full of marbles.

## How to play
Tap a marble, then tap the empty hole **two steps away** in a straight line (up, down, left or right — never diagonally). The marble jumps over its neighbour, and the marble it jumped over leaves the board. Keep going until no jump is left.

- **One marble left:** you won!
- **One marble left in the marked hole** (a small golden ring): the perfect finish.
- **Several marbles left with no jump:** the game ends gently and tells you how many remained.

**Undo** takes back one jump at a time, as many times as you like. **Restart** puts the board back the way it started.

## The boards
- **English board** — 33 holes in a cross. All holes are full except the center. The perfect finish is the last marble in the center.
- **French board** — 37 holes: the cross plus one hole in each inner corner. The top middle hole starts empty, and the perfect finish is the last marble in the bottom middle hole (the center cannot be reached on this board).

## Levels
| Level | Start | Help |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | A board already partly solved (10 to 12 marbles), always solvable down to one | Marbles that can jump are ringed; after you pick one, its landing holes glow |
| Medium | The full board | Same highlights as Easy |
| Hard | The full board | No highlights |

## Tips
- Avoid leaving lonely marbles far from the others: a marble with no neighbours can never be removed.
- Clear one arm of the cross at a time and come back toward the middle.
- Undo is free. Try an idea, and if it goes nowhere, undo and try another.
