# Pyramid

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/pyramid.md) · [Español](../../es/games/pyramid.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/pyramid.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/pyramid.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/pyramid.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/pyramid.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/pyramid.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/pyramid.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/pyramid.md)

A patience game of adding up. 28 cards make a pyramid of seven rows; the other 24 are the deck. Remove **two cards that add up to 13**. Clear the whole pyramid to win.

## How to play

- Card values: Ace = 1, numbers as shown, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13. So Queen + Ace, Jack + 2, 10 + 3, 9 + 4, 8 + 5 and 7 + 6 make 13, and a **King goes on its own**. The values are shown at the top of the screen.
- Only **uncovered** cards can be taken: a card is free when no card lies on it from the row below. Covered cards look a little darker.
- Tap a card, then a second one: if they add up to 13, both leave. The game tells you what the first card is worth and what it needs. Tap a King once to remove it.
- Tap the **deck** (on the side of your thumb) to turn a card; the turned card can pair with a pyramid card. When the deck is empty, tap it again to turn the cards back over, if your level still allows it.
- **Undo** takes back moves. **Hint** shows a pair (or tells you to turn the deck). When nothing more can be removed, the game says so: you can undo, give up or keep looking.

## Levels

Every deal can be cleared if you could go through the deck as many times as you like (the app checks this before you play).

- **Easy**: go through the deck as many times as you like.
- **Medium**: three times through the deck.
- **Hard**: only once through the deck.

## Tips

- Take pairs that free cards in the pyramid before pairs with the deck.
- When you can choose between two equal cards, take the one that uncovers more cards.
- Kings are free points: remove them as soon as they are uncovered.
- On Medium and Hard, look at the whole pyramid before turning the next card: each turn is precious.
