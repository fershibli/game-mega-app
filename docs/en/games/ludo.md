# Ludo

🌐 **English** · [Português](../../pt-BR/games/ludo.md) · [Español](../../es/games/ludo.md) · [Français](../../fr/games/ludo.md) · [Italiano](../../it/games/ludo.md) · [Deutsch](../../de/games/ludo.md) · [Русский](../../ru/games/ludo.md) · [日本語](../../ja/games/ludo.md) · [中文](../../zh-CN/games/ludo.md) · [العربية](../../ar/games/ludo.md)

A race game for 2 to 4 players: you and one, two or three computer players. Each player has four pieces waiting in a yard in a corner of the cross-shaped board. Roll the die, bring your pieces out and take them once around the board, then up your own colored column to the center. The first to get all four pieces to the center wins.

You play **blue** (bottom left) and you start. At the beginning of each game you choose how many computer players sit at the table (1, 2 or 3); the app remembers your choice. Every color also has its own symbol (circle, triangle, square, diamond), so you never need to tell colors apart.

## How to play

- Tap the **die** in the middle of the board (or the **Roll** button).
- The pieces that can move are circled. Tap one of them, or the square where it would land.
- When only one move is possible, the piece moves by itself after a short pause. When no move is possible, the turn passes.
- **Undo** takes back your last choice (with the same number on the die). **Hint** circles a good move.

## The rules

- You need a **6** to bring a piece out of the yard onto your start square.
- Pieces go clockwise once around the board and then into their own column. The center must be reached with the exact number.
- Landing on a square with a rival piece **captures** it: it goes back to its yard. Two of your own pieces may share a square.
- A **6** gives you another roll.

## The rules you can choose

- **Classic**: the start squares and the squares with a **star** are safe: nobody can be captured there. Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
- **Simple**: no safe squares, and no limit on 6s in a row.

## Levels

- **Easy**: the computer moves any piece at random (but never misses its very last move home).
- **Medium**: it captures when it can, then brings pieces out, then moves its leading piece.
- **Hard**: it also thinks about what the next player could roll, and keeps its pieces out of danger.

## Tips

- Pieces on star squares are safe in the classic rules: a good place to wait.
- A rival piece one to six squares behind yours can capture it with the next roll.
- Bringing a new piece out with a 6 gives you more choices later.
