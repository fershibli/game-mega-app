Ten classic games in one small app, designed so older adults can play comfortably: big pieces, clear contrast, zoom on every board and buttons within reach of the thumb.

GAMES
• Sudoku
• Solitaire (Klondike)
• Buraco (with a computer opponent)
• Backgammon (with a computer opponent)
• Mahjong solitaire
• Reversi (with a computer opponent)
• Crossword
• Pipes
• Connect the colors
• Dots

Every game has Easy, Medium and Hard. Puzzles are generated on the phone, so they never repeat, and every one of them can be solved.

EASY ON THE EYES AND HANDS
• Pinch to zoom and move around any board (or double-tap / press and turn with one hand)
• Bigger text and bigger pieces, from 80 % to 160 %
• Normal, high-contrast and color-blind friendly colors, each with a dark mode
• Left-handed mode and one-hand mode
• No timers and no rush

YOUR GAME IS ALWAYS SAVED
Close the app by accident? It asks if you want to continue where you left off.

PRIVATE AND FREE
• No ads, no purchases, no account
• No internet permission and no data collected
• Open source: anyone can read the code and check it

WHY IT EXISTS
Many older and vulnerable people are targeted by games full of misleading ads that make them install apps and spend money without meaning to. Mili Games is the opposite: it is, and always will be, free and without ads.

Made with the help of Claude, the AI assistant by Anthropic. No AI chat or language model runs inside the app.

Source code and documentation: https://github.com/fershibli/game-mega-app

Available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Russian, Japanese and Chinese.
